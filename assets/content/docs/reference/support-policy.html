Release channels

CoreCaseX is free to download and use. The public distribution source is Modrinth. The current 2026.1.1 file is marked beta; release-channel wording follows the distribution metadata rather than an internal file.

Supporter preview builds

Preview, staging and release-candidate builds may be shared for early testing. They can contain incomplete or changing behavior and are not supporter-exclusive stable releases.

Critical fix policy

Security, exploit, data-loss and major crash fixes are not intentionally held behind paid membership. A preview may be used for short validation before a confirmed fix becomes public.

Optional paid support

Membership may cover response priority, setup assistance, configuration review, compatibility troubleshooting, debugging help and preview testing. It does not sell access to the public plugin or unlock in-game features.

Discord authentication

Any membership-authentication bot is for Discord roles and support-channel access. CoreCaseX itself does not require Patreon authentication and does not unlock commands, modules or gameplay behavior through membership.
